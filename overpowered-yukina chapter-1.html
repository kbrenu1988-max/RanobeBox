Chapter 1

Beginning of Ice in the Imperial Academy

The quiet mansion's ice duchess gets ready as the sun rises for a new beginning. Yukina Elarion, daughter of Grand Duke Elarion, rumoured as the ice duchess with the heart of ice, enrolls in the Imperial Academy.


"Got ready for your first day?"

a voice from behind interrogates, Mizuki Elarion, known for his water magic, he is the eldest son of the Elarion household and the elder brother of Yukina. Yukina remaining emotionless as ever, replies

"Is the carriage ready?".

All he could do was stand hopelessly seeing his sister's reactionless face. As she prepares to leave, a cheerful voice shouted

"Make sure to make friends. Okay?"

the cheerful voice was none other than Kazuma Elarion, the second eldsest child of the house and the second elder brother of Yukina. The brothers exchanged eye contacts both wanting their sister to have a best life in their own ways.


Throughout the ride Yukina remains quiet as her thoughts drift back to her childhood when she was always avoided and feared because of her high noble status, her looks and rare ice magic now wonders how her life in the academy would be. Her thoughts come to an end when the carriage arrives at the academy. The chattering of the students stops and whispers began to spread as Yukina enters the Academy.

"Isn't she the daughter of The Grand Duke Elarion?".

"Isn't she the rumoured ice duchess?".

"Is she already fourteen?".

whispers like this spread among the students as Yukina walked down the hallway completely unbothered.

"I have expected this to happen cause this is what I always got whenever I enter a place full of nobles"

thought Yukina as her brothers guided her towards the Entrance Ceremony.

"So that's Yukina Elarion. She seems so different from the rumours"

a voice filled with a warm aura echoed quietly in the ballcony that merely no one noticed.
