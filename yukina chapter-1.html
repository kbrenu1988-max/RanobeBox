Yukina Chronicles

Chapter 1

Beginning of Ice in the Royal Academy

The quiet mansion's ice daughter gets ready as the sun rises for a new beginning. Yukina Elarion, daughter of Duke Elarion, rumoured as the ice duchess with the heart of ice, enrolls in the Royal Academy.


"Got ready for your first day?"

a voice filled with pure overprotectiveness, Mizuki Elarion, known for his water magic, he is the eldest son of the Elarion household and the elder brother of Yukina. Yukina remaining emotionless as ever, replies

"Is the carriage ready?".

All her brother could do was stand hopeless seeing his sister's reactionless face. As she prepares to leave, a cheerful voice shouted

"Make sure to make friends. Okay?"

the cheerful voice was none other than Kazuma Elarion, the youngest child of the house and the little brother of Yukina. The brothers exchanged eye contacts both wanting their sister a best life.


Throughout the ride Yukina remains quiet as her thoughts drift back to her childhood when she was always avoided and feared because of her high noble status and extraordinary ice magic now wonders how her life in the academy would be. Her thoughts come to an end when the carriage arrives at the academy. The chattering of the students stops and whispers began to spread as Yukina enters the Academy.

"Isn't she the daughter of Duke Elarion?".

"She is the ice duchess, Right?".

"Is she already fourteen?".

whispers like this spread among the students as Yukina walks down the hallway completely unbothered.

"I have expected this cause this is what always I always got whenever I enter a place full of nobles"

thought Yukina as her brother guides her to the Entrance Ceremony.

"So that's Yukina Elarion. She seems so different from the rumours"

a voice filled with a warm aura echoed in the ballcony.
